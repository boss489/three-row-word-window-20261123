# Art direction — 세 줄 단어창

A small letterpress puzzle in a warm, nighttime print shop. Cream paper (#F4EBD6) sits on deep teal ink (#173E43), with coral mechanical keys (#D77B61) and brass match seals (#C29244). The background is hand-drawn in Godot: layered paper frame, fine ruling, corner ornaments, a three-pane arched window, tiny sun/cat/dog emblems. Cards are not naked UI rectangles: double ruled frames, clipped corner details, ink shadows and letterpress registration ticks make a coherent desk artifact.

All illustrations and UI primitives are authored in GDScript, with no stock art. Korean captions and Latin capitals use bundled SIL OFL Noto Sans KR (source and license in CREDITS.md); bold target words are tracked uppercase ink. Korean captions must be glyph-checked before release. Each move displaces a row by a short ease-out slide and stamps matched columns with an “OK” mark plus border (not color alone). Buttons are at least 88 Godot units in the 640-unit design width, yielding 44 CSS px at 320px. Text remains legible in that layout. Feedback tones are generated sine-wave PCM at runtime, with a mute toggle. The moving panes, paper, type, stamps, and tones should feel like one press mechanism rather than separate widgets.

States: title has a prominent central window and chapter selector; tutorial shows a live first-row demonstration and requests a real move; play shows three always-visible target cards above three rows with two row-end mechanical arrow keys per row; success seals the completed print; failure shows an over-inked sheet; restart overlays a choice. No images or fonts loaded from a CDN at runtime.
